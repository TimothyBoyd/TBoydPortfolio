STAR‑LAB — Near‑Miss Collision Detection

Active — Project Lead

At SDSU's Hillside Computer Vision group (under Dr. Arash Jahangiri, College of Engineering), I lead a small team building an object-detection and tracking pipeline for drone footage of a shared-street intersection. The goal is automated, camera-based near-miss detection — an alternative to waiting for crash reports to understand where an intersection is actually dangerous.

The pipeline runs a YOLOv8 model across seven road-user classes (car, pedestrian, scooter, bicycle, truck, skateboard, and more), tracked frame-to-frame with a custom class-agnostic tracker I built on top of ByteTrack — it adds per-class confidence floors, streak-based label switching, ghost re-identification, and cross-class duplicate suppression to keep IDs stable through occlusion. A calibration module maps pixel coordinates to real-world distance using hand-clicked reference points matched against surveyed measurements, which feeds a Post Encroachment Time (PET) model that flags near-miss conflicts between road users.

I coordinate the annotation effort in CVAT across two collaborators, manage exports from Roboflow (remapped from alphabetical to the pipeline's canonical class order), and lead task assignment and meetings for the team.

SDHEART — Homelessness 311 Report Analysis

NSF SDHEART Project

Working with Dr. Ming-Hsiang Tsou at SDSU's HDMA Geography Center, I analyzed free-text descriptions from thousands of homelessness-related 311 service reports filed across San Diego County. Topic modeling surfaces the recurring themes inside the reports — camping and encampments, trash and illegal dumping, disturbances near parks and schools, RVs — and each topic is then mapped back to where it clusters geographically and how it trends month over month.

The kernel density layers show unsheltered activity concentrated around the East Village/Gaslamp shelter corridor and the Ocean Beach–Pacific Beach coastline, while the monthly heatmap tracks how individual topics (park/school proximity, dumping, drug-related disturbances) have shifted in volume since 2019.
